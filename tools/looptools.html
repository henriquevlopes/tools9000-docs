LoopTools

Eight tools for working with edge loops, all in one right-click submenu.

What it does

LoopTools gives you quick fixes for loops of edges and vertices: join two loops with new faces, turn a loop into a circle or a curve, even out the spacing, flatten a patch, relax a bumpy loop, or lay a loop along a line you draw. The tools follow the behaviour of Blender's LoopTools, so the options will be familiar if you know it.

Each tool opens a small settings box in the viewport. The result shows live while you change the settings.

How to use it

  1. Open an Editable Poly (or an Edit Poly modifier) in the Modify panel and go to a sub-object level.
  2. Right-click in the viewport and choose LoopTools9000. It is the first item in the lower-left part of the menu and only shows at sub-object level.
  3. Pick a tool from the list.

Options

ToolWhat it does
BridgeJoins two loops with a strip of new faces.
LoftJoins several loops one after another.
CircleTurns a loop into an even, flat circle.
CurveTurns a loop into a smooth curve.
SpaceSpaces the vertices of a loop evenly.
Flatten (LoopTools)Presses a patch of the mesh flat.
GstretchLays a loop along a guide line you draw.
RelaxSmooths out a jagged loop.

Good to know

  • Every tool works on Editable Poly and on the Edit Poly modifier.
  • Every tool shows a settings box in the viewport with OK, Cancel and Apply. Your settings are remembered.
  • LoopTools installs a small plugin: restart 3ds Max once after the setup.
  • The tools are also in the 9000 Toolkit category, so you can put them on a toolbar or give them a hotkey.
