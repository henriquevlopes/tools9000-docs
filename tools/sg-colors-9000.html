SG Colors 9000

Shows the real smoothing groups of the finished object.

What it does

SG Colors 9000 is a small helper modifier used by the Smoothing panel's Colors button. While the colours are on, it sits on top of the stack and paints the smoothing groups of the end result. That way a mirrored side made by Symmetry 9000 shows its own colours, not a copy of the source's.

How to use it

  1. You do not add it yourself. Select your objects and press Colors in the Smoothing panel.
  2. On objects that have modifiers other than Edit Poly, SG Colors 9000 appears on top of the stack.
  3. Press Colors again to turn the colours off. The modifier is removed.

Options

There is nothing to set by hand. The modifier only paints into the colour channel the Smoothing panel gives it.

Good to know

  • It follows changes live, including edits in an Edit Poly lower in the stack.
  • It stays on top when you add more modifiers.
  • Objects with only Edit Poly modifiers work without it.
  • Restart 3ds Max once after the setup so the plugin loads.
