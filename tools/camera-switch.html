Camera Switch

Look through the scene's cameras one after another with one key.

What it does

Camera Switch makes the active viewport look through the cameras in your scene, one after another, then takes you back to the view you came from.

Press Num 0 repeatedly to step the viewport through the scene cameras.

How to use it

  1. Press Num 0 (the zero on the number pad) with a viewport active.
  2. From your own view you go to a camera. Press again for the next one. After the last camera you return to the view you left.
  3. To start at a particular camera, select it first.

Options

There are no settings. Cameras are taken in name order.

Good to know

  • From your own view it goes to the camera you switched to last, or to the first by name.
  • With a single camera it toggles between that camera and your view.
  • A note at the top of the viewport names the camera, or says "back to your view". With no cameras it says so.
  • It remembers the view per viewport, including its position and field of view.
