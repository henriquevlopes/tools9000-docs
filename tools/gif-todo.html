PageWhat to show
3 Point AlignClick three points on an object, then three targets, and it snaps into place.
Align PieOpen the pie, flick Flatten, then use Nudge steps on a few vertices.
ArcRoll the mouse wheel to change the angle while the row bends live.
Camera SwitchPress Num 0 repeatedly to step the viewport through the scene cameras.
Clean UpPress 3 at vertex level, then at polygon level on a box with flipped polygons.
Edge FlowDrag the wheel to change Tension on a row and see the flow update.
Edge Marks PieHold Shift+E, flick towards Make Hard, then drag the crease value live.
Extend BordersRun Extend Borders and change Depth and Angle in the dialog while the mesh grows.
FlattenClick vertices to lock them, toggle options with keys, and watch the selection flatten.
Flatten (LoopTools)Select a bumpy patch and run Flatten: it presses flat onto the plane.
Ghost ViewPress Alt+Z on a selected object: it goes see-through and back.
InsetDrag the mouse to inset a group of polygons, then press D for depth and O for outset.
Knife ProjectPick two shapes, right-click, and watch the cut and the selected inside polygons appear.
Matcap ViewTurn Matcap View on and off on the viewport while orbiting a sculpt-like object.
MirrorRun Mirror on an object, then Shift+click to follow another object as it moves.
Modes PieTab flick between Vertex, Edge, Polygon and Element, keeping the selection.
NudgeMove a selection with the arrow keys and W A S D, changing the step with 1-4.
Reference ImagesDrop images from Explorer into the window and scale them to the model.
ResampleWheel changes the number of points along the row, live.
Save PieHold Ctrl+S and flick to Save, then to Incremental Save.
Smart BevelOne key on edges, then on vertices: the right bevel dialog opens each time.
Smart CreateSelect two facing polygons and press Space: the bridge is created.
SpaceRun Space on a ring with uneven points and watch them even out.
SplinePatchClick Place points on a surface and watch the patch appear at 3 points and follow every new one.
To CircleWheel changes the segments, then K / F / S / R options on a square loop.
To SurfaceHover faces to preview, click to keep, and the vertices slide onto the surface.
UV ToolbarUnwrap, Pack and the checker on a model in the Edit UVWs window.
VTXPainter 9000Paint a vertex colour on a mesh with the brush and assign it to a selection.
