Tools9000 for 3ds Max

A toolkit of modelling tools for 3ds Max: right-click tools, pie menus, UV and smoothing helpers and a few modifiers. Made to be fast, live and calm to use.

Tools

The manual is being written tool by tool. These pages are ready:

InsetLive inset of the selected polygons, with eight options on keys. Knife ProjectCut the outline of a shape into a mesh as seen from the view. MirrorOne click to a live, editable mirror.
More tools are coming: pies, LoopTools, UV tools, Smoothing, Clean Up, SplinePatch and the modifiers.

Requirements

3ds Max 2024 (Windows). Max 2027 support is planned.
