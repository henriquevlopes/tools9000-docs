Tools9000

Model faster in 3ds Max.

Tools9000 is a toolkit of live modelling tools, pie menus, LoopTools, UV and smoothing helpers and a few modifiers. Everything lives where you already work: the right-click menu, the quad menu and a hotkey.

InstallHotkeysBrowse tools

Pies

Flick a key, drag in a direction, done.

Edge MarksCrease, weight, hard and smooth edges. AlignLine up, space, flatten and nudge. ModesSwitch sub-object level with Tab. SaveOpen, save, merge, import, export.

Modelling

InsetLive inset with eight options on keys. Knife ProjectCut a shape into a mesh from the view. MirrorOne click to a live mirror. SplinePatchA live quad patch drawn on any surface. Extend BordersGrow polygons outward from open edges. To CircleTurn loops into round circles. Edge FlowSet Flow, Curve and Linear. Smart BevelOne key opens the right bevel.

LoopTools

Eight tools for edge loops: Bridge, Loft, Circle, Curve, Space, Flatten, Gstretch and Relax.

LoopTools overviewAll eight tools at a glance. BridgeJoin two loops with a strip. RelaxSmooth a jagged loop.

UV and smoothing

UV ToolbarUnwrap, pack, density and checker. SmoothingColours, Optimize, Make Hard. Clean UV ChannelsName and clear UV channels.

Cleanup and view

Clean UpOne key tidies the selection. Check N-gonsOutline and count n-gons. Ghost ViewSee through selected objects. Matcap ViewA clay look for the viewport.

Modifiers and plugins

Symmetry 9000Live mirror and radial modifier. SG Colors 9000Smoothing group colours. Spiral 9000Straps, threads and knurls. VTXPainter 9000Paint and assign vertex colours.
Made for 3ds Max 2024 on Windows. Max 2027 support is planned.
